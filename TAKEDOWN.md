# Hearo Catalog Takedown and Removal Procedure

Last updated: 2026-05-02

## Summary

If you are an artist, rightsholder, or authorized representative and you want a track removed from Hearo Catalog, you have two paths: an informal removal request, or a formal DMCA notice. The informal path is faster and is what most artists use. Both paths are honored.

## Informal removal request

If you are the artist or the rightsholder and you simply want a track removed, send an email to the address below. Include:

- The track title and, if you know it, the catalog ID
- Your name and your relationship to the work
- A short statement that you would like the track removed

We will acknowledge within 48 hours and act within 5 business days. We do not require a formal DMCA notice for this path. We do not require you to swear under penalty of perjury. We do require enough information to identify the work and to confirm you are authorized to ask.

This is the path most artists prefer. It is what The Mod Archive, Modland, AMP, and scene.org have done for decades, and it is what we do.

A valid DMCA notice under 17 U.S.C. Section 512(c)(3) must include:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the rightsholder
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (URL or catalog track ID is sufficient)
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rightsholder

We will acknowledge within 48 hours and act within 5 business days.

## Counter-notice

If a track was removed via a DMCA notice and you believe the removal was in error, you may submit a counter-notice. The counter-notice must include the standard Section 512(g) elements: identification of the removed material, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the removal was a mistake, your contact information, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate federal court.

## What we will and will not do

**We will:**

- Honor any verifiable artist or rightsholder request promptly
- Remove the file from Cloudflare R2, remove the catalog entry from the manifest, and ensure the track does not return in any future revision unless you tell us otherwise
- Treat informal requests with the same urgency as formal notices
- Maintain a private log of all requests received and actions taken

**We will not:**

- Re-list a removed track unless the original rightsholder explicitly grants permission
- Argue the merits of the underlying rights claim with you. If you say it is yours and you want it down, it comes down. If a counter-claim arrives later, we follow the standard counter-notice process.
- Charge any fee for a takedown or removal request
- Publish your contact information without your consent

## Public log

We maintain a redacted log of takedown notices received and actions taken, modeled on github/dmca. This log will be published in this repository under `dmca/` once the first notice is processed. Entries include the date, the requesting party (with contact information redacted unless they consent to publication), the work involved, and the action taken. The intent is transparency, not exposure.

If you are an artist and you would like to be in the catalog (rather than out of it), or if you have questions about our editorial standards or rights posture, see COPYRIGHT.md and CONTRIBUTING.md, or email the address above with subject line "Hearo Catalog general inquiry".
